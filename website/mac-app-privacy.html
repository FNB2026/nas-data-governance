NDG Mac App Store 版隐私政策

适用于筹备中的 NDG Mac App Store 首发版。实际可用状态以 Mac App Store 为准。

本机处理

NDG 读取你通过 macOS 选择器授权的本机或已挂载目录。文件名、路径、元数据、内容指纹、扫描进度及分析结果存放于本机项目。目录授权书签也保存在应用容器内。授权源为只读。

应用没有账户、广告、跟踪和遥测功能。应用不向开发者传输扫描内容或项目数据。已挂载网络共享由 macOS 与你配置的服务器通信。你主动打开支持链接时，浏览器会访问对应网站。

你可以通过关闭项目和 macOS 的应用数据管理控制本机数据。提交支持请求时，请勿发送源文件、数据库、授权书签或未脱敏的路径。

Privacy Policy for NDG on the Mac App Store

This policy describes the planned first Mac App Store edition. Availability is shown in the Mac App Store.

NDG reads local folders or mounted volumes you choose through macOS. File names, paths, metadata, fingerprints, scan progress and results stay in your local project. Folder access bookmarks are stored in the app container. Source access is read-only.

The app has no accounts, ads, tracking or telemetry. It does not transmit scanned content or project data to the developer. macOS communicates with the network shares you configure. Support links open external sites in your browser.

You can manage local project data on your Mac. Please omit source files, databases, access bookmarks and unredacted paths from support requests.
